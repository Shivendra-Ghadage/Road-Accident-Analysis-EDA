# Key Findings — Road Accident Analysis 2025

## Dataset Overview

The analysis is based on 101,525 reported road collisions from the
2025 UK road accident dataset.

## 1. Accident Severity

- 74,881 collisions were classified as Slight.
- 25,191 collisions were classified as Serious.
- 1,453 collisions were classified as Fatal.
- Serious and Fatal collisions together represented 26.24% of all
  recorded collisions.

## 2. Monthly Pattern

- November recorded the highest number of collisions with 9,188.
- February recorded the lowest number of collisions with 7,155.
- Accident frequency varied across the months of 2025.

## 3. Day-of-Week Pattern

- Friday recorded the highest number of collisions with 16,811.
- Accident frequency differed across the days of the week.

## 4. Part-of-Day Pattern

- Afternoon recorded the highest number of collisions with 35,488.
- Accident frequency varied considerably across different periods
  of the day.

## 5. Road Type

- Single carriageways recorded the largest number of collisions.
- A total of 74,150 collisions were recorded on single carriageways.

## 6. Speed Limit

- The 30 mph speed-limit category recorded the highest number of
  collisions.
- It accounted for 49,673 recorded collisions.

## 7. Weather Conditions

- Fine weather with no high winds was the most common recorded
  weather condition.
- 83,405 collisions occurred under this condition.

## 8. Light Conditions

- Daylight was the most common recorded light condition.
- 72,814 collisions occurred during daylight conditions.

## 9. Road Surface

- Dry road surfaces were the most common recorded surface condition.
- 75,679 collisions occurred on dry surfaces.

## 10. Urban vs Rural

- Urban areas recorded more collisions overall than rural areas.
- Urban collisions: 67,148
- Rural collisions: 34,373
- Rural areas had a higher fatal collision rate of 2.71%, compared
  with 0.78% in urban areas.

## 11. Vehicle Involvement

- Most collisions involved one or two vehicles.
- Two-vehicle collisions were the most common category with 61,869
  collisions.

## 12. Casualties

- The median number of casualties per collision was 1.
- The average number of casualties was approximately 1.26.
- One unusually high record contained 142 casualties and was retained
  for investigation rather than automatically removed.

## Overall Insight

The analysis shows that collision frequency is concentrated in
specific time periods, road types, and speed-limit categories.

At the same time, rural collisions show a higher fatal collision rate
than urban collisions.

These findings identify areas that may benefit from further road
safety investigation.
