# Business Recommendations — Road Accident Analysis 2025

## 1. Prioritize High-Frequency Periods

Road safety resources and awareness campaigns can be targeted toward
periods with consistently high collision frequency.

The analysis shows that Friday and afternoon periods recorded high
collision volumes.

## 2. Focus on Single-Carriageway Roads

Single carriageways represented the largest share of recorded
collisions.

These roads can be prioritized for detailed safety assessments,
including road design, traffic management, visibility, and speed
management.

## 3. Strengthen Rural Road Safety

Rural collisions had a higher fatal collision rate than urban
collisions.

Further investigation could focus on:

- Road geometry
- Speed management
- Visibility
- Roadside hazards
- Emergency response time
- Driver behaviour

## 4. Investigate Speed-Limit Categories

The 30 mph category had the highest collision count.

However, collision count should not be interpreted as collision risk
without traffic-exposure data.

Speed-limit categories with higher Serious/Fatal rates should also
be investigated separately.

## 5. Do Not Focus Only on Bad Weather

Most collisions occurred under Fine weather, Daylight, and Dry road
conditions.

Therefore, road safety strategies should not focus only on extreme
weather or poor visibility.

## 6. Combine Frequency and Severity

Future road safety analysis should consider both:

- Number of collisions
- Severity rate

A category with many collisions is not necessarily the category with
the highest risk of fatal or serious outcomes.

## 7. Investigate Major Collision Events

Records with unusually high casualty counts should be reviewed
individually to understand the circumstances of major incidents.

## 8. Use Geographic Analysis

Future analysis can combine collision coordinates with geographic
information to identify accident hotspots.

## 9. Add Traffic Exposure Data

Collision counts alone do not measure risk.

Adding traffic volume, vehicle miles, road length, or other exposure
measures would allow more meaningful risk comparisons.

## 10. Build a Monitoring Dashboard

A Power BI dashboard can be used to monitor:

- Collision volume
- Serious/Fatal rate
- Monthly trends
- High-risk road types
- Rural vs Urban severity
- Weather and light conditions
- Vehicle involvement
- Casualty patterns
